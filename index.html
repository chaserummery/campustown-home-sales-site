What this can tell us

Patterns are not explanations

This dataset can show whether sale prices and campus distance move together within a university market, and whether farther high-priced homes tend to have more living area or different recorded features. It cannot establish why buyers paid more: it does not measure neighborhood desirability, renovation quality, views, or many other influences. Reported home type is especially unreliable, so it is not used as an explanation.
